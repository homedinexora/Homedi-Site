2.2 Data collected automatically

  • Location: your home-base location (latitude/longitude) for discovery; while you toggle yourself "live", your availability and location make you discoverable to patients within approximately 2 km; during an accepted visit, your live location may be shared with the booked patient for arrival coordination. Location is not tracked when you are offline.
  • Device and app data: push-notification token (Firebase), app version, device type, crash diagnostics.
  • Security and audit logs: login attempts (with hashed technical identifiers), OTP events, and an append-only audit trail of actions on the Platform.

2.3 Verification data from third parties

To verify your eligibility we query official registers - the National Medical Commission / Health Professional Registries for doctors and the State Nursing Council registers for nurses - and record the verification outcome. Your listing is activated only after successful verification, and we may re-verify periodically.

2.4 What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your bank or payout details: consultation fees are paid to you directly by patients in cash/UPI and never pass through the Platform.
  • Personal data never appears in system logs; logs reference internal record identifiers only.
  • We do not sell personal data and do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. What Patients and Others Can See

  • Patients within your discovery radius see your professional profile: name, provider type and category, specialization, languages, consultation fee, locality/apartment-complex proximity badge, distance, and verification status. Your exact home address and phone number are not shown in discovery.
  • A booked patient additionally receives arrival coordination information, including your approach location during "on my way", for that visit only.
  • Your registration number may be displayed on prescriptions you issue, as required by prescribing regulations.
  • We may indicate that a provider was removed from the Platform, without publishing reasons, except as required by law or a regulator.

5. Consent - and How to Withdraw It

  • At registration we present an itemised notice and request your consent by clear affirmative action.
  • You may withdraw consent at any time from Settings → Privacy & Data or by writing to the contacts in Section 11. Withdrawal is as easy as giving consent. Because discovery, verification, and booking cannot operate without the associated data, withdrawal of consent for core processing will be treated as a request to delist; processing before withdrawal remains lawful.
  • Going "offline" in the app immediately stops discovery and location processing without affecting your account.
  • Communication preferences: transactional messages (booking alerts, OTPs, policy and safety notices) are part of the service and continue while you are listed. Promotional messages are sent only with your separate, optional consent, withdrawable at any time in Settings; we honour TRAI DND preferences for promotional content.
  • You can review and update your profile information at any time in the app; if you cannot correct something in-app, contact providers@[●] or the Grievance Officer. Changes to credential data trigger re-verification.
  • Once Consent Managers registered under the DPDP Rules are operational, we will support consent management through them.

6.2 Regulators, government, and others

  • Professional councils / registries: we query NMC/HPR/SNC registers to verify you, and may report credible findings of impersonation, practice without registration, or serious professional misconduct to the relevant council where required or permitted by law.
  • ABDM/National Health Authority: if you link an ABHA, verification is performed through ABDM systems; prescriptions you issue may be shared to the patient's ABDM PHR at the patient's option.
  • Legal: disclosure where required by law, court order, or to state instrumentalities as permitted under the DPDPA; and to protect the safety of patients and users.
  • Corporate events: on merger, acquisition, or reorganisation, data may be transferred to a successor bound by this Policy and the DPDPA, with notice.

7. Data Residency and Security

  • All personal data is stored in India (AWS Mumbai region, ap-south-1).
  • Sensitive fields (phone number, name, date of birth, email, ABHA number) are encrypted at the field level; data in transit is protected by TLS.
  • Access to production data is role-restricted and recorded in an append-only audit trail archived to tamper-evident (write-once) storage.
  • OTP login is rate-limited; sessions use short-lived signed tokens revocable on logout.
  • Application logs contain no personal data - internal identifiers only.

8. Data Retention and Erasure

  • We retain your personal data while your account is active and as long as necessary for the stated purposes.
  • After delisting or account deletion: profile data is removed from discovery immediately. Records of completed visits, prescriptions you issued, and related audit entries are retained for the periods required by medical record-keeping law, tax law, and the DPDP Rules, then erased or anonymised.
  • Erasure requests: on a valid request we erase or anonymise your personal data and instruct processors to do the same, except where retention is required by law - noting that prescriptions and visit records also constitute patients' health records and are retained accordingly.
  • Inactivity: if the DPDP Rules' user-inactivity erasure obligations apply to your account class, we will erase data after the prescribed period of inactivity, with at least 48 hours' prior notice.

9. Your Rights as a Data Principal

Under the DPDPA you may, free of charge, via Settings → Privacy & Data or the contacts in Section 11:

  • Access a summary of your personal data, processing activities, and recipients;
  • Correct and complete inaccurate or outdated data (note: changes to credential data trigger re-verification);
  • Erase personal data, subject to legally required retention (Section 8);
  • Raise grievances and receive a response within prescribed timelines - including grievances about verification outcomes, no-show records, or suspension;
  • Nominate a person to exercise your rights on death or incapacity;
  • Withdraw consent at any time (Section 5).

We verify identity via your registered phone number before acting on requests. Unresolved grievances may be escalated to the Data Protection Board of India as prescribed under the DPDPA.

10. Personal Data Breach

If a personal data breach affects you, we will notify you without delay through your registered communication channel - describing the breach, its likely consequences, mitigation taken, and recommended steps - and will notify the Data Protection Board of India in the form and timelines prescribed by the DPDP Rules (initial intimation without delay, detailed report within 72 hours).
