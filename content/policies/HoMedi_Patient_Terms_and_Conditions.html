6. ABHA — Optional Linking

Linking an ABHA number to your HoMedi account is entirely optional and voluntary. The Platform is fully functional without ABHA: discovery of Providers, booking, and receipt of prescriptions work identically whether or not you have linked an ABHA.

  • ABHA linking is available from the Profile and Settings screens, and may be offered after a completed visit. It is verified by an OTP sent by ABDM to your ABDM-registered mobile number.
  • If you link a verified ABHA, digital prescriptions issued by doctors on the Platform can additionally be shared to your ABDM Personal Health Record in the FHIR standard, with your consent.
  • You may unlink your ABHA at any time from Settings; unlinking does not affect your HoMedi account or bookings.

9. Home Visits — Your Obligations

  • Provide an accurate, complete visiting address and be present (or ensure the dependent Patient and a responsible adult are present) at the scheduled time.
  • Ensure a safe, respectful, and non-threatening environment for the Provider. Harassment, violence, intoxication-related misconduct, or unsafe premises entitle the Provider to terminate the visit without refund of the Booking Fee, and may lead to account suspension and reporting to lawful authorities.
  • For examinations of a minor or of any patient who requests it, a chaperone/responsible adult should be present. For any examination, you may request that a family member be present.
  • Disclose relevant medical history, allergies, and current medications truthfully to the Provider. Withholding material information can endanger your safety.
  • Use the Platform's visit-tracking features lawfully. The Provider's live location shared before a visit is for coordination only.
