1. Scope of This Policy

This Refund & Cancellation Policy ("Policy") governs cancellations, rescheduling, refunds, and no-shows for home-visit bookings made on the HoMedi platform operated by NeXora Health Systems Private Limited, CIN U86909TS2026PTC215305, registered office at 4-12-927/1,PlotNo.E1,RD-1, Portion-C, Sai Nagar Colony, Vanastalipuram, Hayathnagar, Hyderabad- 500070, Telangana ("NeXora", "we", "us"). It forms part of, and should be read with, the Terms & Conditions - Patients and the Terms & Conditions - Healthcare Providers.

Only one payment is ever made through the Platform: the ₹50 Booking Fee, paid online via Razorpay at the time of booking. The Consultation Fee (₹450 for an MBBS doctor, ₹950 for a Specialist, ₹350 for a Nurse) is paid by the patient directly to the provider at the patient's home, in cash or by UPI, only after the visit is completed. Because NeXora never collects the Consultation Fee, all refunds under this Policy relate solely to the ₹50 Booking Fee. If a visit does not take place, no Consultation Fee is due to anyone.

3. How to Cancel

  • Cancel through the app: Bookings → select the booking → Cancel. The cancellation time recorded by the Platform (Indian Standard Time) is decisive for the 2-hour rule.
  • If you are unable to cancel in-app, contact support at support@nexorahealthsystems.in. The time your request is received is treated as the cancellation time.
  • A booking for a dependent may be cancelled by the patient or by the caregiver who booked it.

4. Rescheduling

  • Where the app offers rescheduling, a reschedule requested more than 2 hours before the visit carries the Booking Fee over to the new slot at no extra charge.
  • A reschedule requested less than 2 hours before the visit is treated as a late cancellation followed by a new booking, and the original Booking Fee is forfeited.
  • We do not guarantee that the same provider, or any provider, will be available at the rescheduled time. If no slot is available, you may cancel under Section 2.
  • NeXora or the provider may propose a reschedule (for example, provider running late). If you decline a reschedule proposed for reasons not attributable to you, you receive a full refund.

5. Refund Method and Timelines

  • Refunds are made only to the original payment method used at booking, through Razorpay. Refunds are never made in cash or to third-party accounts or instruments.
  • Refunds are initiated automatically when the qualifying event occurs (eligible cancellation, provider cancellation or no-show, or platform cancellation) - no separate request is needed.
  • Once initiated, refunds normally reach your account within 5-7 working days, depending on your bank or card issuer, and in any event within 15 business days. UPI refunds are typically faster.
  • Failed or duplicate payments: amounts captured for bookings that were never confirmed (including payment-gateway failures and duplicate debits) are refunded automatically through Razorpay's standard reversal process.
  • Fallback (NEFT): if a refund to the original payment method fails (for example, a closed account or expired instrument), our support team will contact you to collect your bank account number, IFSC code, account holder name, and branch, and process the refund by NEFT. Please ensure these details are accurate; NeXora is not responsible for transfers that fail or are misdirected because of incorrect details provided by you, though we will assist in tracing them.
  • Promotional credits or vouchers (if offered in future) have no cash value: amounts paid using such credits are re-credited as credits, not as money.
  • If a refund you are expecting has not arrived within the timelines above, write to support@nexorahealthsystems.in or the Grievance Officer with your booking ID; we will provide the Razorpay refund reference and assist with tracing.

6. What This Policy Does Not Cover

  • Consultation Fees paid to providers. These are paid directly to the provider after a completed visit and are outside the Platform's payment system. A dispute about a Consultation Fee (for example, an alleged overcharge above the displayed fee) should be raised through the grievance process; NeXora will investigate and may take action against the provider, but does not itself hold or return that money.
  • Medicines, consumables, diagnostics, or other purchases made from third parties or from the provider outside the booked visit.
  • Dissatisfaction with clinical outcome. Completion of a visit by a verified provider does not entitle the patient to a refund of either fee on the ground of the clinical result. Complaints about the quality of care are handled through the grievance process and, where applicable, the provider's professional regulator, without prejudice to the patient's rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.
  • Stored records. Records of completed visits - including prescriptions - are stored by NeXora as a technology service under the Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policies, with the patient's consent. Cancellation of a booking or processing of a refund does not affect records of visits already completed, which are retained in accordance with the applicable Privacy Policy and medical record-keeping law.

7. Abuse of This Policy

Repeated patterns of late cancellations, no-shows, chargeback abuse, or bookings made without intent to avail the service may result in warnings, booking restrictions, or suspension of the account (patient or provider), in accordance with the applicable Terms & Conditions. Chargebacks raised for amounts already refunded, or in place of the grievance process for service disputes, may be contested with the payment network.
