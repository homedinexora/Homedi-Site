6. Professional Conduct and Clinical Responsibility

  • You must comply at all times with all laws and professional regulations applicable to your practice, including (as applicable) the NMC's ethics and professional-conduct regulations, State Medical Council rules, Indian Nursing Council / State Nursing Council codes, the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 and rules, and the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985.
  • You must practise strictly within your registered scope. Nurses must not diagnose independently or prescribe medicines, and must escalate to a doctor or hospital where clinically indicated.
  • You must obtain informed consent for examinations and procedures, respect patient dignity and privacy, and accommodate a patient's request for a chaperone/attendant during examination. Examination of a minor requires a parent/guardian present.
  • If a patient's condition exceeds what can be safely managed in a home setting, you must advise and facilitate referral or emergency transfer; do not undertake care beyond the safe limits of a home visit.
  • You must arrive within the scheduled window, carry professional identification, and conduct yourself professionally. Attending a visit under the influence of alcohol or drugs is grounds for immediate termination.
  • You may decline or terminate a visit where your safety is at risk, and should report the incident through the app.

7. Prescriptions

  • Prescription functionality is enabled only for verified doctors. It is technically and contractually disabled for nurses.
  • Prescriptions must be generated through the Platform's prescription module, which produces a digital PDF delivered to the patient (and, where the patient has linked a verified ABHA and consented, a structured FHIR copy to their ABDM Personal Health Record).
  • Every prescription must reflect your independent clinical judgment following an in-person examination during the booked visit, must comply with applicable prescribing regulations (including generic-name prescribing norms and rules on Schedule H, H1, and X drugs), and must be accurate and legible.
  • You must not issue prescriptions without examining the patient, pre-sign blank prescriptions, or prescribe outside your scope or registration. You are solely liable for every prescription you issue.
